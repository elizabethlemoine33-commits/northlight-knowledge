# Trust Is the Operating Handrail

## Statement

Operating systems depend on trust. Trust is not a soft supplement to process — it determines whether people can use process honestly.

## Why It Matters

A methodology cannot manufacture trust. When trust is weak, even a technically sound methodology can become a reporting exercise, a compliance burden, or a source of political behaviour. When trust is strong, a relatively simple mechanism can create meaningful accountability and empowerment.

In a trusted environment, people can acknowledge that a priority is not working, ask for help before a problem becomes a crisis, own work without constant supervision, and report incomplete work honestly. Without trust, operating systems become performative.

## Evidence

- `../../../evidence/northlight/eos-rocks-okr-retrospective.md` — OKR implementation suffered from insufficient organisational trust; Rocks succeeded in part because the team could report honestly on misses.
