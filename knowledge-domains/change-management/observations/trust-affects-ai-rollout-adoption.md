# Trust affects AI rollout adoption

## Observation

AI ROI and adoption articles connect successful rollout to whether employees understand the use, accountability, and response to unexpected outcomes.

## Interpretation

Trust is part of adoption infrastructure because people need to know what will happen when outputs are wrong, risky, or challenged.
