# Public strategy only matters when translated to next actions

## Observation

Articles on Canadian AI strategy focus on what a small business can actually do now, not on the full national policy narrative.

## Interpretation

Large strategic announcements become useful to operators only when translated into concrete decisions, eligibility, timing, and next steps.
