## Career Arc

### Northlight Advisory Services — Founder (Mar 2026–present)

Practice covering strategy, AI adoption, operations, and applied AI tools. Three production tools shipped:

- **Northlight Vault** — Windows app unifying Google Drive, OneDrive, and Dropbox in a single read-only browser; Electron + direct OAuth 2.0, no stored credentials
- **Northlight Meetings** — Windows call-recording and analysis app; captures mic and system audio, transcribes via Groq Whisper, extracts action items via Llama 3.3, routes output to Drive/ClickUp/Calendar
- **Northlight OS** — AI-native operating system for the practice: connects ClickUp, Gmail, Google Workspace, Slack, and Claude into one system, automating task execution, content pipeline, and daily briefings

Also part of the **Go Fractional** network (Jul 2026–present) as Fractional COO / Director of Operations, connecting with early-stage founders and leadership teams (8–30 people) building their first operational layer.
