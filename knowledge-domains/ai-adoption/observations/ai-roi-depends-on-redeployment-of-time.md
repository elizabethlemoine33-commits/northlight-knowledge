# AI ROI depends on redeployment of time

## Observation

The website corpus treats hours saved as incomplete unless the freed time is connected to a better use.

## Interpretation

AI value appears when capacity is redirected toward higher-value work, better decisions, or reduced friction rather than merely recorded as time saved.
