## Summary

Most leaders have an AI strategy. The problem isn't the plan—it's the planning cadence. Why annual strategy breaks when capability moves quarterly.

## Key Claims

- The Treebeard problem: AI capability moves on a quarterly or sub-quarterly cycle; annual strategy planning produces plans that are already outdated before they are deployed
- Annual AI planning cycles are structurally misaligned with the pace of AI development — a strategy written in Q1 is not reliable guidance for Q4
- One named person — not a committee — should own the "what changed in AI this quarter?" question; diffused ownership means nobody tracks it
- 15 minutes in a quarterly leadership meeting is enough to maintain genuine AI strategic awareness if done consistently — the investment is not the problem, the cadence is
