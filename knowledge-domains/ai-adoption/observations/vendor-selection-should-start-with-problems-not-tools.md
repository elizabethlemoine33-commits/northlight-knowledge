# Vendor selection should start with problems, not tools

## Observation

AI advisor and implementation partner articles repeatedly warn against providers who lead with tools before understanding the business problem.

## Interpretation

Tool-first vendor selection increases the risk of buying a solution before the underlying operational need is known.
