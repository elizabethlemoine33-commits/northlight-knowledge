## Key late-interview shift

The interview began focused on the relationship between operations and marketing.

It expanded into a broader practitioner philosophy about:
- interconnected systems;
- organizational sense-making;
- adaptive capacity;
- trust;
- learning;
- accountability;
- cross-functional integration;
- and building for what the organization is becoming.

The framework should preserve the original market-awareness insight without reducing the broader philosophy to marketing.
