# Effective Advisory Starts with Diagnosis

Clients typically arrive with a solution already in mind. The advisor's first job is to suspend that and understand the operating situation beneath the request. Stated service categories and presented solutions routinely obscure the actual need.

The quality of what an advisor can offer is proportional to how well they understand the specific context of the client — and context builds over time. Early investments in diagnosis pay compounding returns across an engagement.

This understanding should continue to accumulate supporting evidence.
