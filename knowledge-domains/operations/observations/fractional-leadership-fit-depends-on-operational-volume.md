# Fractional leadership fit depends on operational volume

## Observation

Fractional COO content frames the decision as a fit question based on operating volume, complexity, and stage rather than status.

## Interpretation

A full-time senior operator may be unnecessary or premature when the organisation needs structured leverage more than a permanent executive seat.
