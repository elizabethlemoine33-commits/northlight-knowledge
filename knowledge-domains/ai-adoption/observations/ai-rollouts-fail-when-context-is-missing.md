# AI rollouts fail when context is missing

## Observation

Published AI adoption articles repeatedly describe weak outcomes as a context problem rather than a model problem.

## Interpretation

The recurring issue is not that the tool cannot perform in general. The problem is that it cannot see the organisational knowledge, decisions, constraints, and relationships needed to produce useful work.
