## Key Claims

- 98% of organisations have employees using unapproved AI tools; 78% of AI users bring their own tools to work — they are solving problems now rather than waiting for IT provisioning
- IBM 2025 Cost of a Data Breach Report: shadow AI-involved breaches cost an average of $4.63M ($670K more than breaches without unsanctioned tools); average detection time 247 days — a clarity failure, not a technology failure
- Three responses that fail: banning (drives behaviour underground and marks problem-solvers as rule-breakers), writing a 40-page policy (the people already asking permission keep asking; the others still don't), waiting to see (agentic AI wave means this gets harder, not easier)
- Shadow AI audit = 4 questions: What AI tools are people actually using right now? What data are they putting in, specifically? Who is the actual human who can say yes or no? What is the one next decision to make?
