## Key Claims

- PIPEDA applies the moment an AI system processes any personal information about an identifiable individual — names, emails, purchase history, employee records, IP addresses; it does not distinguish AI from traditional data processing
- The question before the compliance question: "Why are we collecting this information?" PIPEDA governs how you handle data you've decided to collect; this question governs whether you should be collecting it at all
- Quebec Law 25 is significantly stricter than PIPEDA: requires a Privacy Impact Assessment before deploying AI systems processing personal info, 72-hour breach notification to Quebec's CAI (vs. PIPEDA's "as soon as feasible"), explicit opt-in for profiling
- By default, most consumer AI tools route data through US servers — PIPEDA does not prohibit cross-border transfer but requires disclosure and equivalent protection; most consumer AI terms of service don't include the data processing agreements PIPEDA accountability requires
- Canadian data residency options: Azure Canada Central, Google Cloud Vertex AI (Montréal), AWS Bedrock (Canada West)
- You cannot contract out of PIPEDA accountability by pointing to a vendor's terms of service — you remain accountable for data you hand to third-party AI systems
