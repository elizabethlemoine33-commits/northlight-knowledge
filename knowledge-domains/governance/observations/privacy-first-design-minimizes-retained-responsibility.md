# Privacy-first design minimizes retained responsibility

## Observation

The Vault article presents not collecting user data as a deliberate design choice rather than a missing analytics feature.

## Interpretation

Privacy-first design reduces obligations by limiting what the system collects, stores, and therefore becomes responsible for protecting.
