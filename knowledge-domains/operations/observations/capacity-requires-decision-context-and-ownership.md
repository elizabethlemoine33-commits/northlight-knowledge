# Capacity requires decision context and ownership

## Observation

Multiple posts describe capacity problems as failures of context, ownership, decision rights, or structure.

## Interpretation

People cannot carry work independently when key knowledge, risk interpretation, or decision authority remains trapped with the founder or leader.
