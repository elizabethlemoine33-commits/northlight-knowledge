## Key Claims

- Three MCP skills on MCP Market: Morning Briefing Pro ($19) for daily orientation before work starts, Strategic Voice Framework ($29) for brand voice consistency and drift detection, MCP Market Publisher ($29) for standardising the skill listing process
- All three started as internal tools built for a one-person advisory practice; they became products only after regular use proved they were genuinely useful, not just functional
- MCP Market was chosen because the infrastructure (Stripe integration, listing pages, discovery) already exists — not building a payment system was the right stage-appropriate decision
- Target user: solo operators, consultants, and fractional professionals managing many responsibilities who can't spend cognitive energy on orientation, voice consistency, or publishing process every day
