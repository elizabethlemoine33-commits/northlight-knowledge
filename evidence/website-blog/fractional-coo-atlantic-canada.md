# Fractional COO Services in Atlantic Canada — Northlight

## Summary

A practitioner-written guide to fractional COO work specifically in the Atlantic Canada market — written because most available content targets Toronto or US businesses. Argues that the fractional model fits Atlantic Canada's specific conditions: smaller talent pool, tighter margins, relationship-based business culture.

## Key Claims

- A fractional COO does the same work a full-time COO does — systems, roles, strategy-to-execution — but at 10–20 hours per week on a part-time basis. Not a consultant who leaves a deck; owns the work and the outcomes.
- Atlantic Canada's market context makes full-time COO hiring a bigger risk: smaller candidate pool, relationship-based culture, tighter margins. Fractional de-risks the commitment.
- The comparison is not fractional vs. nothing — it is fractional vs. continuing to do the work yourself, hiring wrong, or paying $15,000 for a report that sits in a drawer.
- Cost range: $6,000–$16,000/month in Atlantic Canada (lower end of the $8,000–$18,000 North American range), versus $280,000–$305,000/year fully loaded for a full-time COO.
- Fit profile: 10–75 people, $1M–$15M revenue, owner-operator still carrying operations by default.
- Not a fit for: enterprise-scale (150+ people), pre-revenue businesses, situations requiring daily physical presence.
- About 80% of fractional COO work is remote; in-person when it adds value (workshops, planning sessions).
- Goal of the engagement is to make the fractional COO unnecessary: build systems and structures that hold after they leave.
