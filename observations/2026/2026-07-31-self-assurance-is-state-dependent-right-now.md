# Observation

## Context

While reviewing the CliftonStrengths 34 profile on 2026-07-31, Elizabeth confirmed
Self-Assurance (#16) as accurate — "very confident who I am as a person, generally, generally"
— but flagged, unprompted, that her current state doesn't match the trait: an extended period out
of work has put her self-assurance "at its lowest... in a long time."

## Observation

CliftonStrengths measures stable natural talent, not current mood or circumstance. A theme can
be genuinely dominant in someone's talent profile while being harder to access or express during
a difficult season. Elizabeth drew this distinction herself, unprompted, while fact-checking her
own report against a claim Claude had gotten wrong about a different theme (Command) — trusting
her own read of her current state over what a rank number alone would imply.

## Why It Matters

Any writing, resume, or interview material that draws on confidence or self-assurance as a
strength should stay accurate to the trait level — real, durable, evidenced across her career —
without implying she feels that way day-to-day right now. Extended unemployment is an ordinary
and sufficient explanation on its own; this note treats it as a factual, dated data point, not a
clinical concern.

## Questions Raised

- Worth revisiting once the job search resolves, to see whether the state read changes.
