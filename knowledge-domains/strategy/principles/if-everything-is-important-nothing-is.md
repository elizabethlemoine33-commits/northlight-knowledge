# If Everything Is Important, Nothing Is Important

## Statement

A functioning operating system must give an organisation a way to say not now. Prioritisation is not ranking things from most important to least important — it is deciding what deserves attention at this moment.

## Why It Matters

Without a mechanism for deprioritisation, organisations accumulate parallel activity. Every department can be busy while the organisation becomes less aligned. The parking lot is not a graveyard — it is a deliberate holding area for ideas that are real but not current.

For any proposed piece of work: Is it important now? Does it advance what we are currently trying to accomplish? What would we stop or defer to make room for it? If the answer to either of the first two questions is no, the idea may belong in the parking lot rather than the active workstream.

## Evidence

- `../../../evidence/northlight/eos-rocks-okr-retrospective.md` — EOS Rocks implementation explicitly distinguished between Rocks, important-but-not-a-Rock work, and parking-lot ideas. OKR implementation lacked a parking-lot mechanism and suffered from inability to deprioritise.
