# AI strategy cadence must match capability change

## Observation

Several articles argue that annual AI strategy is too slow for a capability environment that changes quarterly or faster.

## Interpretation

AI strategy needs a review rhythm that matches the pace of change in tools, risk, organisational readiness, and opportunity.
