# AEO requires structured evidence, not just keywords

## Observation

The AEO article treats AI search visibility as an entity, structure, evidence, and citation problem rather than classic keyword optimization alone.

## Interpretation

AI discoverability improves when public knowledge is structured clearly enough for answer engines to identify, distinguish, and cite it.
