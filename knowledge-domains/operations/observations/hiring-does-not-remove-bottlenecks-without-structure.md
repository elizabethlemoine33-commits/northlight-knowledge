# Hiring does not remove bottlenecks without structure

## Observation

Operations articles describe scenarios where hiring a qualified person does not remove the bottleneck because ownership, context, or structure is still missing.

## Interpretation

Capacity is not created by role filling alone. The work must be structured so someone else can truly own and execute it.
