## Comfort in Complex and comfort in Chaotic are separate skills

**Source:** `knowledge-domains/leadership-decision-making/observations/comfort-in-complex-and-chaotic-are-separate-skills.md`
**Suggested format:** Blog post or long LinkedIn piece
**Why it's strong:** Has real, specific first-hand stories (on-the-job injury, fall, fire) — not an abstract claim. Evidence-backed in the literature (Cynefin, Uhl-Bien's Complexity Leadership Theory). The manufactured-urgency point — leaders who declare "chaos" to grab centralized authority past the point the actual emergency ended — is the sharpest part and the most publishable.
**Angle:** The common label "handles uncertainty well" bundles two very different skills. A leader can score high on one and low on the other. The diagnostic question isn't whether someone is comfortable with uncertainty — it's *which kind*.
**Constraint:** No naming of specific workplaces, colleagues, or identifying incident details. Bare-bone-lesson framing only.
