# Field Notes — 2026-07-26

## On Maslow and work

Believe in Maslow's Hierarchy. Less certain whether work is the right context to solve for the higher needs — belonging, esteem, self-actualization. It may be that work handles the lower tiers well (safety, stability, some social connection) and the upper tiers need to be found somewhere else. This is not a settled view — flagged as a late-night thought. Worth returning to with more sleep and a proper interview question.

The interview question is already in the file. The answer might be different for founders vs. the people they lead — founders may use work for self-actualization in a way that employees rarely do or should.

## On clarity as a principle that extends to AI

The observation that the two-stage AI workflow (exploratory session → clean brief → fresh execution agent) is an application of "clarity is kindness" — you don't ask the execution agent to sort through your thinking, you hand it a clean brief and let it do what it's good at. This appeared as an insight mid-conversation rather than a designed observation.

## On the contextual relationships distinction

The main thought from tonight — captured properly in OBS-SOC-0008 and updated in interview-questions.md. Short version for reference:

The distinction is not between portable and non-portable relationships. It is between where the context was built: in the institution (borrowed, doesn't survive) or in the bond (built, travels). Best-friend example: neighbours → far apart → 3-4 times a year → pick up where they left off. Client relationship design: learn the person, not just the problem; send the occasional non-work message; the context shouldn't only be the work.

Parallel to systems thinking: systems must outlast their designers (context built into structure, not a person's head). Relationships designed to last must outlast their institutional context (context built into the bond, not the employer).
