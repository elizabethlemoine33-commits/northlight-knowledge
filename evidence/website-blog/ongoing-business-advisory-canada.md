## Key Claims

- Ongoing advisory is a monthly retainer with a thinking partner who builds context over time — closer to a trusted board member than a project consultant who delivers a report and leaves
- Clearest signal you need ongoing advisory: you are regularly making important decisions — about strategy, team, clients, growth — with no one to pressure-test them
- Canadian pricing: light retainer $1,500–$2,500/month (4–6 hours), standard $3,000–$5,000 (8–12 hours), deep fractional $5,000–$10,000+; day rates $1,500–$3,500
- Full-time COO in Canada costs $120,000–$200,000/year in salary alone; a $3,000/month retainer at the same experience level is 18–36% of that cost for a fraction of the headcount
- Structure a 90-day pilot before committing to a long-term retainer — defines success, protects both parties, and gives the relationship enough context to become genuinely useful
