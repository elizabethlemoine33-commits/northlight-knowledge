# AI Adoption Is Operational Before It Is Technical

Organisations that lead with model selection or automation scope consistently underperform those that lead with context, readiness, and outcome measurement. AI tools work in proportion to the organisational knowledge they can access and the operating conditions they are deployed into.

ROI is not found in hours saved. It is found in whose time was freed, to do what, with what resulting capability.

This understanding should continue to accumulate supporting evidence.
