# Knowledge Is an Operational Asset, Not Documentation

Organisations that treat knowledge as a documentation task produce archives. Organisations that treat it as an operational asset produce systems that work — for people and for AI. The difference is structure, ownership, and maintenance discipline.

Context is infrastructure. AI tools perform in proportion to the structured, current knowledge they can access. An organisation with well-maintained knowledge systems gives AI a usable working environment. One without context forces AI to guess.

This understanding should continue to accumulate supporting evidence.
