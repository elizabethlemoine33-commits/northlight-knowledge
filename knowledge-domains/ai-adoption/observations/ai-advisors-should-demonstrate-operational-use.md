# AI advisors should demonstrate operational use

## Observation

The corpus distinguishes casual AI use from building working AI-enabled workflows, pipelines, or tools.

## Interpretation

Credible AI advisory requires evidence that the advisor understands operational implementation, not only prompts, demos, or strategy language.
