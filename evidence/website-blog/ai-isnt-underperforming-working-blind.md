# Your AI Isn't Underperforming. It's Working Blind. — Northlight Advisory Services

## Summary

Your AI rollout didn't fail because of the tool. It failed because your AI can't see the organisational context it needs to work. Here's how to fix it.

## Key Claims

- Five context layers your AI can't see: conversational (what you said in the session), structured data (what your AI has in its database), process (how your workflows are documented), knowledge (what your org knows and how it's stored), organisational (strategy, goals, constraints)
- The context gap is an organisational problem, not a technical one — most AI underperformance is because the AI lacks access to organisational context, not because the tool is insufficient
- Before concluding an AI tool doesn't work, audit what context it actually has access to — the tool can only see what you show it
- Practical fix: context provisioning is the real implementation work, not tool selection or prompt writing
