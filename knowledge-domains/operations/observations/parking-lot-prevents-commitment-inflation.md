# Parking Lot Prevents Commitment Inflation

## Observation

Without a deliberate holding mechanism for ideas that are real but not current, organisations tend to accumulate active commitments until everything is a priority and nothing is actually advancing.

## Interpretation

The parking lot is not a rejection — it is an acknowledgement that an idea has value without creating an obligation to pursue it now. It allows teams to say "not this quarter" without losing the idea permanently. Operating systems that lack this mechanism tend to see scope creep, overcommitment, and stalled priorities. The discipline of deciding what belongs in the parking lot is itself a form of strategic prioritisation.

## Evidence

- `../../../evidence/northlight/eos-rocks-okr-retrospective.md` — EOS Rocks implementation developed an explicit parking-lot practice; the later OKR implementation lacked it and suffered from an inability to challenge or deprioritise proposed objectives.
