# Resistance Is Evidence, Not Obstruction

Change fails most often not because people are unwilling but because they cannot see where they are going, why it matters, or what their role will be on the other side. Resistance is diagnostic: it points to incomplete communication, missing involvement, or an unclear destination.

Small frictions compound. An adoption barrier that looks minor in a pilot becomes a blocking problem at scale. Involving people early — not to inform them but to shape the change with them — converts resistance into ownership.

This understanding should continue to accumulate supporting evidence.
