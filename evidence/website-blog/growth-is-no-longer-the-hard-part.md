## Summary

When AI makes work faster, who benefits? The distribution question at the heart of AI adoption—for countries and 40-person businesses alike.

## Key Claims

- The distribution question: if AI generates capacity and speed, the new constraint is not growth but who benefits from that capacity and how it gets directed — true at the macro and business level simultaneously
- Dario Amodei's essay on AI and economic prosperity frames the same question at a country scale; Anthropic committed $350M to Economic Futures research on AI's economic distribution impact
- For small businesses, the AI question shifts from "can we grow faster?" to "what do we do with the time AI gives back, and are we routing it toward the right work?"
- Growth is no longer the hard part — the distribution of new capacity and the decisions about what to build with it are the new hard parts
