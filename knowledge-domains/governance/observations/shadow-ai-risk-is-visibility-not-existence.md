# Shadow AI risk is visibility, not existence

## Observation

Shadow AI is described less as a discipline failure and more as a visibility, ownership, and data-flow problem.

## Interpretation

The existence of unofficial AI use matters because leaders do not know what data is moving where, who owns the outcome, or what risks are being created.
