# Plans need checkpoints before sunk cost speaks

## Observation

The Day 200 article argues that constraints often become visible only after work is far enough along to meet reality.

## Interpretation

Plans need regular checkpoints so new evidence can revise the path before sunk cost begins defending the original plan.
