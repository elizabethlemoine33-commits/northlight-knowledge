## Key Claims

- AEO Phase 1 (Foundation): structured data markup, entity disambiguation, FAQ blocks, consistent entity naming across all properties
- Gemini has a serious entity collision problem — confuses Northlight Advisory Services with Northlight Solutions Group; ChatGPT is the most accurate; Perplexity is directionally correct where it has data
- The citation gap is a content channel problem, not a technical one — AI engines cite where they already have publisher sources, so the fix is distribution not crawlability
- robots.txt AI crawler warnings are a false positive — blocking AI crawlers does not prevent AI citation or recommendation; the two pipelines are separate
